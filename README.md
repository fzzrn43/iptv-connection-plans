This is one of those IPTV terms that sounds more technical than it really is.

A connection basically means **one simultaneous stream**. TiviLive explains that one connection allows one screen at a time, while a 2-, 3-, 4- or 5-connection plan is intended for households that want multiple screens running at once.

So imagine a fairly normal evening:

The TV in the living room has a football match on. Someone else is watching a movie on another screen. A third person has decided that the evening belongs to them and is watching something completely unrelated.

That is where multi-connection iptv üyelik becomes relevant.

If you only ever watch on one screen at a time, the 1-connection plans are the simplest place to start.
